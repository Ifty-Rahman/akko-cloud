Configure your Akko PC75B

Lighting, key remapping, macros and keyboard settings — straight from the browser. No vendor app required.

Connect your keyboard

Plug the keyboard (or its 2.4 GHz receiver) into this computer. Needs Chrome, Edge, Opera or Brave on desktop.

Connecting didn’t work? and send it along with your report.

Just looking?

Explore every screen with a simulated keyboard. Nothing is written to real hardware.

Select a key above to remap it.

Custom per-key colours

Brush Click or drag across keys to paint.

Keys or LED strip dark? Press Fn + L on the keyboard; the driver can’t see that switch. Changes are saved even while the lights are off.

Backup & restore

Save every profile (key maps, Fn layers, lighting, options) plus the macros your keys use to a file, and restore it later or on another PC75B.

Keyboard shortcuts

Built into the PC75B; they work without any driver. From the Akko PC75B manual.

Debug log

For troubleshooting: turn on Record packets, reproduce the problem, then download the log and send it along with your report. It keeps the last 3000 packets, with notes on what the app was doing, in this tab only.
